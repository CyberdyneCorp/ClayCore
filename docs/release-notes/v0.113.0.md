# claycore v0.113.0

Covers minors 0.104.0 through 0.113.0. The previous tag is v0.103.0, whose
draft release was never published — see **Upgrading**.

**Read this one if you PICK, if you MIRROR, if you EXPORT, or if you drag.**
Four of the changes below fix silent wrong output rather than slowness.

## Four defects that produced wrong answers, not slow ones

**A per-brick tape declared a Lipschitz bound for deformers it did not emit**
(#541). `cull_deformers` drops every grab whose support cannot reach a brick —
over that region it is the identity — but `fold_info` separately folded the
item's *whole* chain. A brick emitting nine grabs of forty-eight declared
`1.125^48 = 253.61` where the honest figure is `1.125^9 = 2.93`.

That is not only slow. `craycast` computes `h = f(p) * step_scale` and accepts
when `|h| < eps * t`, so the step scale scales **the distance the hit test
sees**. A bound two orders too large makes the marcher accept hits on rays that
miss the shape. A 64×64 fan into a worked cap at 48 dabs, against a dense
sign-change truth of 3960 hits:

| | hits | truth | time | steps |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| bound over the item's chain | **4090** | 3960 | 759.2 ms | 3,511,660 |
| bound over the emitted chain | 3960 | 3960 | **48.0 ms** | 226,458 |

**15.8× faster and 130 false picks removed.** In a host those were picks
landing on nothing, or at the wrong depth, with nothing reported. Every version
before this one has it.

**A layer's symmetry could be set but never read** (#538). A host that cannot
ask has to remember, and an undo invalidates the memory without telling it.
Reproduced by a host: an unmirrored dab, an undo, the same dab again, and the
far side grew **0.28 world units** on a stroke asked to be unmirrored.
`clay_document_layer_mirror` and `clay_document_layer_radial` make that class of
desync unreachable rather than fixed — read, compare, set only if it differs.

**An export could be non-manifold, and nothing said so** (#567). `decimate`
could return edges carrying four incident triangles from an input that had none,
and `clay_document_mesh` documents itself as *the watertight, 2-manifold export
path* with decimation reachable through `clay_mesh_params.decimate`. Measured on
a sphere-minus-box across four voxel sizes and five ratios, **4 of 20
configurations** came back non-manifold. The sharpest case — two crossed tori
meshed at 0.035, decimated to a quarter — produced two edges of incidence four
and moved the Euler characteristic from **-4 to -2**: the collapse closed a
handle, and the non-manifold edges are the scar.

meshoptimizer chooses its own collapses and does not apply the link condition
`collapse_edge` refuses on. `decimate` now checks its own result and retries with
a different choice of collapses **at the requested size**, taking a clean result
only if it did not grow the mesh. All four measured failures recovered that way,
within two triangles of what was asked.

Where no collapse order is clean it returns the requested size and **says so**,
through the new `DecimateReport`. It does not hand back the undecimated input:
that was the first version of this fix and it turned a requested 12,418 triangles
into 155,388 on `examples/37_groups`. Downstream, a pinched mesh is the class of
defect that breaks slicers and boolean engines while looking correct in a
viewport — so if you export, read the report.

**The brick mesher emitted sliver triangles, and they cost a host the cull**
(#549). 2,297 in 83,464 on the reference fixture. A sliver's face normal is a
cross product of near-parallel edges, so it shades black wherever gradient
normals are unavailable — and the fix for that is the expensive part, described
under **Faster** below.

## What moves under you

### The entry points

```c
clay_document_layer_mirror        /* read a layer's mirror back (0.105.0) */
clay_document_layer_radial        /* read its radial array back (0.105.0) */
clay_layer_move_surface_regions   /* a drag reports where it reached (0.106.0) */
```

Nothing was removed. Every struct change is an append behind `struct_size`.

**`clay_move_params` grew twice**, and the order matters to a caller compiled
against an intermediate version: `gesture_id` (0.107.0) then `steady` (0.109.0),
appended in that order. A caller passing the older `struct_size` is zero-filled
and unaffected.

### The answers that changed

**`advises_consolidation` now fires on a layer it used to decline** (#534). It
was `degraded && volumes`, so a single drawable carrying a brush chain was never
advised — on the authority of a measurement that consolidation is 6× *worse*
there. That measurement is not disputed; it names a regime, and a real session
goes far past it:

| dabs | step scale | parametric | baked | ratio |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 6 | 0.147973 | 17.05 ms | 9.01 ms | 1.89x |
| 8 | 0.078267 | 37.47 ms | 9.03 ms | 4.15x |
| 16 | 0.006126 | 202.06 ms | 10.24 ms | **19.73x** |

At 16 dabs the parametric arm scores **0 hits of 1844** — past that depth the
field renders *wrong*, not slowly. A host reading the flag simply starts getting
`true` below a step scale of 0.125.

**`clay_brick_cache_mesh_lod` answers gradient normals at a level** (#550),
where it used to refuse them. At lod 0 they still come from per-brick culled
tapes; at a level they come from the **whole-document tape**, so a level's
attribute pass no longer follows the bricks named. That cost is taken
deliberately: measured on a worked sphere, `CLAY_NORMAL_FACE` on a coarse
lattice is up to **84.78°** from the field where the gradient reads 0.00°, and a
coarse surface was face-shaded by construction.

**Per-vertex colour is still refused at a level**, for a reason the gradient does
not share: a mip carries no colour lattice and nothing supplies one.

**A drag with a moving radius or centre is now one gesture** (#533), if the host
says so. `clay_move_params.gesture_id` names it; matching used to be raw float
equality on centre and radius, so a pressure-driven radius cost **101×**:

```
anchored                    2 warps   0.121 ms
pressure-driven radius    120 warps   5.726 ms    101x
finger-following centre   120 warps   5.740 ms    101x
```

Leave it zero and the old rule applies exactly.

**`steady` suspends a promise, at non-zero only** (#532).
`clay_sdf_move_update` states that updates of 0.1, 0.2 then 0.5 end exactly
where a single fresh 0.5 does. Lazy-mouse lag is path-dependent by definition,
so above zero that cannot hold. At zero the path is bit-identical.

### The formats

**THE KERNELS ARTIFACT MOVES, and a host pins it by release tag.** #543 changes
kernel math: the circ easings are held short of their singularity, so their
values differ by up to ~1.4%. Every other curve is unchanged. If you validate
against `claycore-kernels.zip`'s host parity fixture, its circ rows will not
match the previous release's.

That was a soundness fix, not a tuning change. `E'(t) = t/sqrt(1-t^2)` is
unbounded as t → 1, so the declared slope — 39.98 against a measured 90.50 —
was **below** the truth, and a bound below the truth lets the marcher step
through the surface. The singular argument is reached at a grab's centre.

No scene-format change. `Deformer` gained `gesture_id`, deliberately **not
serialised**: it names a gesture in flight, and a loaded document reads zero and
falls back to the previous rule.

The device baseline changed for `pose_region` only (#539) — see below.

## Faster

- **A Move chain's declared bound fell 12.44×** (#542) — `ease_max_slope` returned
  a 512-point sample times a blanket 1.25× for every curve, linear included,
  whose true slope is exactly 1. At 48 grabs: 1,443,067.88 → 116,008.14.
- **Culled-tape raycast, 15.8×** at 48 dabs (#541), above.
- **Whole-document tape, 1.33×** at 8 dabs. Stated separately because it is the
  path a host picks through when it does not cull, and it is the smaller number.

### If you re-mesh the whole document to hide specks, STOP — that is now unnecessary

Stated plainly because a host reading "slivers fixed" would refresh its golden
images and keep the workaround: the workaround lives in host code and the defect
lived here.

A host that could not show the slivers (#549) re-meshed the **whole field** after
every completed stroke rather than the bricks the stroke touched.
`clay_document_mesh` evaluates densely with **no cull**, so its cost tracks the
document instead of the edit:

| | 1 dab | 48 dabs | degradation |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `clay_document_mesh` | 2.8 ms | 26.2 ms | **9.40×** |
| `clay_brick_cache_mesh` | 4.2 ms | 6.3 ms | 1.49× |

Confirmed independently from the host side on a different fixture: 14.59 → 57.48
ms across 48 dabs, while the mesh it produced grew 4.7% in vertices.

Dropping the whole-field re-mesh removes the host's own settle cost with it — one
host measured its stroke-end settle at 29.35 ms, of which 13.96 ms was a full GPU
relayout forced by replacing every key at once. On this release both halves go,
not just ours.

## What did NOT get faster, and is worth knowing

**The engine is a minority of the reported stall, and the first published
figure for it was too small.** A live Move drag on a clean one-item sphere,
refilling and meshing every frame, costs the engine **23.7 ms — 3.95 ms/frame**
against an application's 203.4 ms for the same brush. The engine is about
**11.6%** of it.

An earlier version of this note said 4.9%, and that number was a ratio between
two different documents. The engine side was measured at `voxel_size` 0.05 and
the application side on a host running 0.02 with `band_voxels` 3 — **1,728
bricks against 17,576**. Engine cost tracks brick count, so the mismatch
flattered us:

| fixture | engine, whole drag | share of 203.4 ms |
|---|---:|---:|
| voxel 0.05, band 2 | 9.9 ms | 4.9% |
| voxel 0.02, band 3 (the host's own) | **23.7 ms** | **11.6%** |

**And 11.6% is itself the share AT THE FLOOR.** Every figure either side has
quoted was measured on a clean one-item sphere with nothing accumulated — 203.4
ms was "first stroke, isolated by undo". The complaint that opened #531 is
"almost a second after 3 or 4 move dabs", and a host's own probe puts the
deformer chain at 48 after eight. Chain depth is engine cost: every sample in
`eval_grid` walks the chain to carry its point back to rest space, so a chain of
48 is 48 inverse warps per sample across every brick a refill touches.

| chain depth | engine, whole drag | ms/frame | share of 203.4 ms |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 23.7 ms | 3.94 | 11.6% |
| 8 | 29.4 ms | 4.89 | 14.4% |
| 16 | 38.3 ms | 6.39 | 18.9% |
| 32 | 52.3 ms | 8.72 | 25.7% |
| **48** | **69.6 ms** | **11.60** | **34.2%** |

At the depth the complaint actually occurs, the engine is about **a third** of
the cost rather than a twentieth, and 11.6 ms/frame is most of a 60 Hz frame on
its own.

**This is a meshing cost, not the step-scale decay.** `safe_step_scale` is read
once, in `raycast` (`backends/cpu/cpu_backend.cpp:236`); `eval_grid`, `eval_rows`
and `eval_points_batch` never read it. The decay is a separate failure with its
own symptom — the form stops rendering past 16 dabs, 33 ray hits of 512. What
makes *meshing* more expensive as a sculpt accumulates is the chain length
itself.

`benchmarks/stroke_floor_probe.cpp` takes `CLAY_PROBE_VOXEL`, `CLAY_PROBE_BAND`
and `CLAY_PROBE_CHAIN`, so all of the above is one binary rather than a series of
edits.

The conclusion keeps its direction at the floor — most of a first stroke is
host-side, and #531's attribution stands there — but "4.9%" understated the
engine's share by 2.3x at depth 1 and by **7x at depth 48**, and should not be
quoted.

The degradation *rate* across dabs is essentially unchanged (16.41x -> 16.96x
over 8 dabs). These releases lowered the curve; they did not change its shape.

## Known limits, stated rather than discovered

- **No parity scene or deformer golden uses a circ easing**, which is how a
  change to kernel math (#543) left the whole suite green. Those curves are
  unverified on Metal — the same shape as #535, one family over. Their values
  are pinned in `test_ease_slopes.cpp` because nothing else pins them.
- **The TAPE mesher's crossings are deliberately unguarded** (#549). The guard
  that removed the slivers is on the brick path only. `mesh_lattice` and
  `mesh_lattice_parallel` are bit-identical to the previous release, because the
  weld and remesh fixtures produce degenerate triangles on purpose to exercise
  the welder, and a global clamp broke 15 of their assertions.
- **A decimation pinch that no collapse order clears is NOT rare, and such a
  result is returned pinched** (#567). At an aggressive ratio merging sheets is
  what the ratio means: `examples/37_groups` at 0.08 pinches on every retry. A
  caller that cannot use a non-manifold mesh must pass a `DecimateReport` and
  decide — re-run gentler, repair downstream, or accept it. Nothing substitutes
  a different mesh for the one asked for.
- **The 1.05x ceiling on a retry's growth is a judgement, not a measurement.**
  Every recovery observed came in within two triangles of the requested count,
  so no fixture exercises the space between.
- **A Move chain's declared bound is still ~10^5 times the field's real
  gradient** on an *unculled* tape (#541 carried forward). 116,008 declared
  against a measured 4.958 at 48 gestures. #541 fixed the culled path; the
  whole-document path legitimately carries every grab.
- **Every SDF brush stalls 63.6–244.6 ms on a clean one-item sphere** (#530,
  #531). Now attributed: ~88% is host-side, and splitting it needs the host's own
  per-call instrumentation.
- **The regional consolidation closure never stayed local on any fixture tried**
  (#540). Open research, unchanged.
- **First-touch cost may have genuinely grown** — `cut_passes`' first stamp reads
  2.78× its baseline and is unstable run to run. Moved into #531; not measured
  directly yet.

## Upgrading

1. **If you PICK on a heavily-moved layer, take this release.** #541 removed
   picks that landed on nothing. No action beyond upgrading.
2. **If you cache a layer's mirror, stop.** Read it with
   `clay_document_layer_mirror` and reconcile. A cache is what an undo
   invalidates behind your back.
3. **If you invalidate after `clay_layer_move_surface`, switch to
   `clay_layer_move_surface_regions`.** Under symmetry your own box either
   misses the reflected image or becomes the slab between them. Size the buffer
   from the layer's symmetry; a short one is refused *before* anything is
   applied.
4. **If you offer a pressure-driven radius or a following centre on Move, set
   `gesture_id`.** Without it each frame is a new warp — 101× measured.
5. **If you read `advises_consolidation`, expect more `true`.** It now fires on a
   degraded deformer-only layer below a step scale of 0.125.
6. **If you recorded device figures as fixtures, `pose_region` moved.** Its
   baseline was 8.7× above anything measured across seven gate records; budget
   and measurements are re-derived from the band.
7. **If you validate against the kernels artifact, re-baseline its circ rows.**
   #543 changes those three curves by up to ~1.4% and nothing else.
8. **If you set `steady` above 0.95, it is now refused.** Both lazy-mouse
   gestures share the ceiling `clay_stroke_resolve` already clamped to, so one
   value can no longer mean two things. That range never shipped.
9. **If you EXPORT with decimation, take this release and read the report.**
   #567 could hand you a non-manifold mesh silently; it still can hand you one
   at an aggressive ratio, but `DecimateReport` now tells you. Re-test any
   exported asset a downstream tool rejected or repaired — it may not have been
   the tool. A decimated result may differ by a triangle or two for the same
   options; the size you asked for is the size you get.
10. **If you pin rendered images of brick-cache meshes, refresh them.** #549
    moves vertices by at most 5% of a voxel. Topology does not change — the
    marching case index comes from sign tests alone, so the triangle count is
    identical — but a pixel comparison does not know that, and will go red on an
    improvement. Refresh rather than bisect.
11. **If you re-mesh the whole document after a stroke to hide black specks,
    delete that path.** See **Faster** above; it costs 9.40× against 1.49× at 48
    dabs, and it is no longer buying anything.
12. Nothing else requires action. No format change, no re-layout, no removal.
