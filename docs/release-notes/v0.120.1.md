# claycore v0.120.1

A patch release. Compared against **v0.120.0** (published 2026-09-18), the
previous and latest published release. It covers the eleven PRs merged since
that tag (#635–#638, #645–#648, #651–#653) and the version bump. **No C ABI
symbol was added or removed, and no struct was re-laid out** (see "The ABI").

**Read this if your document has an INFINITE REPEAT GRID, if you drive UNDO on
voxel sculpt layers or count undo steps, if you refill bricks from
`clay_document_undo_bound` / `_redo_bound` or `clay_layer_node_influence_bound`,
if you size meshes by `resolution=`, or if you call pyclay's multires Layer
brush.** Every change below reaches a caller through an entry point it already
calls. Nothing announces them: no version number moved for any of them beyond
the patch digit.

Unless a line says otherwise, every number here is a count or a byte comparison
taken on a Mac from a `cpu-only` build. Timings name their host where the PR or
proposal names one, and say so where it does not. **None of them is a device
number.**

---

## An infinite repeat grid reports an unbounded `tape.bounds` (#645, issue #640)

`tape.bounds` is where the field can hold material: what the mesher marches,
what a raycast clips against, and what `clay_tape_info` tells a host to plan
bricks over. For an item repeated on an **infinite grid** it reported one cell,
although the copies fill space. On main just before the fix (`9616286c`), a 0.4
half-size box on a 1.5 grid minus a radius-1 sphere, sampled on 32³ points over
five cells either way: **5,824 of 5,824 material samples fell outside the
reported box**. The sphere removes cell zero completely, so the box held no
material at all. Every entry point that compiles a tape (`compile_document`,
`_part`, `_except`, `compile_layer`, `_prefix`, `compile_item`, and a resumed
`compile_document_append`) reported the same.

Now, wherever an infinite grid reaches the result **unconfined** — alone, in a
union, or with something subtracted from it — `clay_tape_info` reports
`-FLT_MAX` / `+FLT_MAX` on every axis, as it already did for a plane. A lattice
intersected with something finite is bounded by that thing, and a finite shape
minus a lattice keeps the shape's box. The box is infinite rather than capped,
because any finite box is wrong for a field with material in every cell.

What a lattice document gives up, because every consumer already treated an
infinite box the way it treats a plane:

- `clay_mesh`, the quad mesher, `clay_voxel_rasterize`, the bakes and pyclay's
  `Volume.from_document` / `moved_topologically_from` / `flattened_from`
  **refuse** with "unbounded scene; pass a region" (pyclay: "pass bounds=").
  Before, they quietly worked on one cell.
- **`clay_sdf_smooth_begin` refuses the layer**, as it refuses a plane's. Before,
  it laid its working lattice over cell zero and left every other copy out of
  the transaction.
- Raycast (CPU, Metal, CUDA) and pick skip the AABB clip, which had been
  removing hits on every other copy; pick also skips the per-ray local tape.
- `advised_params` gives no consolidation advice, and the SDF prefix cache
  declines, so the layer is walked in full.

A random-document probe linked against each tree found 1,269,305 of 1,419,282
material samples outside the box on main (518 lattice documents) and **0** on
the fix, across every entry point; documents with no infinite grid planned
byte-identical brick sets (5,942,131 and 5,854,037 bricks over 1,000 documents,
and the nine gallery documents bit-identical). The PR names no host model; the
build was macOS (`cpython-311-darwin`), `cpu-only`.

## Bounds narrowed per operator (#637)

`tape.bounds` used to be the plain union of item bounds. It is now folded by
one rule at every level (item, group, layer fold, and a resumed append): a
**subtract keeps its left operand's extent**, an **intersect keeps the
overlap** (or the left operand when gated or when the boxes are disjoint), and
everything else unions with the right operand dilated by its blend ring. **A
smooth group now adds its own blend ring**, which it did not before, so a box
can also grow. No safe step changed.

Measured on an **Apple M2 Max, Release**, median of 200 meshings at voxel 0.02:
an r0.5 sphere carved by an r2.0 cutter marched **9,640,000 → 125,000 cells**
and meshed **42.18 → 5.26 ms**; two unit spheres intersected, 14.30 → 9.63 ms;
the union control unchanged (37.97 → 38.00 ms). Triangle counts agree within
0.1%.

**A mesh sized by `resolution=` gets finer on the same resolution**, because
pyclay's `Document.mesh(resolution=)` counts cells along the longest side of
`tape.bounds`. The gallery's `37_groups` went 12,418 → 28,846 triangles at
resolution 88 and **746 KiB against a 400 KiB gallery budget**; it now asks for
58 (12,534 triangles, 324 KiB). `30_trim_curve` went 232 → 7,574 at resolution
72. At a fixed voxel size nothing was lost: 202,624 → 203,020 triangles on
`37_groups` at 0.02, same extent.

## Undo reports the grab, not its node (#648, issue #639)

A host Move is one grab per segment at the head of a node's chain, and
`clay_document_undo_bound` / `_redo_bound` reported the union of what each
command targets — for a grab, its node. Every grab widens that node's bound by
its pull, so undoing one segment cost bricks-in-the-node × chain length.
ClaySpaceDesktop measured it against v0.120.0 in a live session: undo wall time
**20 ms → 4,536 ms** over 50 undo steps at a flat ~283k triangles (the host's
own numbers, host machine not stated).

Now, when the chains before and after a `SetDeformersCmd` differ only in a
**head** of grab, magnify, blob or alpha links — links that are exactly the
identity outside their balls — the step reports those balls (a grab's at its
centre AND its displaced end), placed through every mirror, radial copy,
repetition and instancing layer, dilated by each enclosing group's blend
support and every layer fold above, and **clamped into** the node's bound, so it
is never larger than before. Clamped, not intersected: a ball just past the
node's face still reports that face, because the band a consumer adds can reach
it.

Radius-1.5 sphere with N grabs around its equator, one radius-0.15 grab on the
pole undone, voxel 0.05, 8³ bricks, C-ABI probe:

| grabs in the chain | bricks, main before the fix | bricks now | undo p50, before → now |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 1,000 | **12** | 1.665 → 0.089 ms |
| 10 | 1,440 | **12** | 2.481 → 0.086 ms |
| 40 | 4,000 | **12** | 7.415 → 0.115 ms |

Timings: **Mac (M-series, model not named), CPU backend, `cpu-only` Release**,
200 undos per point after five discarded, the two builds interleaved. The
brick count is what the tests pin; the clock is not the claim. **The per-brick
price still grows with the chain**: the same 12 bricks cost 7.4 µs each at 1
grab and 28.1 µs at 160 (same host), though none of those grabs reaches them.
This removes the node-extent factor, not the chain-length one.

## An edit is bounded by the smooth combines after it (#653, issue #650)

`node_reach_bound` dilated a node's bound only by its enclosing groups. But a
node is the running value wherever it is the nearest thing, and a **later**
smooth sibling reads the running value out to its support and carries the
change back into the band. Two r = 0.3 spheres 0.3 apart, the second smooth at
k = 0.3, the first moved 0.1: on main before the fix, **6,359 band samples moved
outside the box plus the band, worst 0.0438, and a cache dirtied by
`clay_brick_cache_mark_dirty_nodes` kept 25 stale bricks**. Now 0 and 0.

So `clay_layer_node_influence_bound`, `clay_brick_cache_mark_dirty_nodes`, the
command path and the undo bound (through #648's clamp) **widen** by the widest
later smooth sibling's full blend support. Nothing widens for a node followed
only by hard combines — every node appended last (a stroke's dabs) and every
document without a smooth blend is bit-identical. On a 400-trial randomized
oracle, bricks refilled rose **2.5%** (plain) and **2.8%** (rich). A per-chain
memo keeps the walk linear: a drag frontier over 1,428 warps of a 10,000-item
smooth layer 0.39 → 0.45 ms, and undoing a 2,134-warp Move 0.85 → 1.04 ms (the
PR and proposal name no host).

## A full brick build matches the raw field where the deformer cull cannot judge (#652, part of issue #649)

`Compiler::cull_deformers` dropped grabs that reach a brick in two cases: a
**repeated item**, whose chain runs on the point folded into its repetition
cell while the cull tested the unfolded one (so a brick over any copy except
the source cell was filled undeformed); and a **grab behind another point
warp** (magnify, twist, pose), which moved the point without widening the
region. A repeated item now keeps its whole chain, and the first non-grab link
ends the test. On the issue's probe, rich random documents with a brick build
off the raw field went **62 → 35, worst 0.2632 → 0.0825**; plain documents
(no repeats) are unchanged at 21, worst 0.0310. **#649 is not closed** — see
Known limits. The per-brick cost of keeping a repeated item's whole chain was
not measured.

## Voxel sculpt layers are on the undo history (#647, #651, issue #642)

**Five voxel sculpt-layer operations are now undo steps** (#647):
`clay_voxel_set_sculpt_layer_strength`, `_visible`,
`clay_voxel_move_sculpt_layer`, `clay_voxel_remove_sculpt_layer` and
`clay_voxel_merge_sculpt_layer_down`. Through v0.120.0 none recorded anything,
and an undo after a dial reverted the older pass onto cells the dial had
already moved while the slider stayed at the new value. Each step now carries
the property AND every cell the recompose rewrote, and replays without
recomposing, so undo is bit-exact. `clay.h` used to list removing a sculpt
layer and merging one down as operations that destroy history; they no longer
are.

**Creating a voxel sculpt layer, and every edit made while one records, are
steps** (#651). On main just before the fix (with #647 in), through the C ABI: begin a layer,
one inflate, undo — the layer still listed **110** cells, and dialling it to 0.5
put 54 undone cells back (2,251 occupied against 2,197); a journal replayed onto
a snapshot older than the layer was refused after 2 of 3 events. Now 0 cells,
2,197 against 2,197, identical document bytes, and 5 of 5 events applied. **A
pass inside a new layer now takes two undos** (the edit, then the layer).
Undoing past the begin ENDS the recording; redoing it brings the layer back
CLOSED, so a host mid-pass that undoes past the begin must begin again.

Two group-bracket defects fixed with them (#647):

- **An unmatched `clay_document_end_undo_group` is a no-op.** It used to fold
  every step since the session began into one — reached by enabling undo
  mid-gesture, where the gesture's begin was refused while undo was off.
- **A nested `begin_undo_group` no longer moves the outer group's fold start.**
  A nested group undid in two steps; groups are now depth-counted and only the
  outermost folds.

**Enabling undo mid-session starts an EMPTY history and is never refused.**
That was already the behaviour — `clay_document_enable_undo` builds its history
on first call — and #647 decided to keep it and document it rather than refuse
or record a barrier. What is new is the documentation, and the advice that
comes with #641: the crash journal a mid-session enable starts is paired with
the snapshot the document was last loaded from or saved to, so **save once
right after enabling mid-session**.

## Multires: the Layer brush in pyclay, the rim, and a stale rebind (#636, #646, #638)

- **pyclay's multires Layer brushes had a zero layer height and moved nothing.**
  `MultiresSculptor.stamp("layer", ...)` (#636, issue #627) and
  `SculptLayerStroke.stamp("layer", ...)` (#646, issue #628) now take
  `layer_height`, default 0.05 (the engine's), as the last keyword. On a 6x6
  plane at level 1: `layer` moved **0** classes where `draw` moved **37**; both
  now move 37. The C entry points never had the gap: `read_mesh_brush` turns a
  zero `layer_height` into the default.
- **The layered smooths averaged a rim vertex over half its ring** (#646). On a
  regionally refined hierarchy, `LayeredMultiresSculptor::smooth` in
  `DetailOnly` and `PreserveDetail` took a rim vertex's neighbours from the
  level's own adjacency, which stops at the rim. On a 6x6 cage with the middle
  2x2 at level 3, one dab of radius 0.9: **64 of 64** rim vertices were off the
  dense hierarchy (worst 0.00347 and 0.0196); now 0. The code was unchanged
  from v0.120.0 up to this fix, so v0.120.0 has it. Uniform hierarchies and
  vertices off the rim are unchanged.
- **A multires sculptor rebinds after a restructure** (#636). Its fast path kept
  a stroke's records whenever the sculpt level's NUMBER was unchanged, but they
  are indexed by vertex. Removing the top level and refining level 3 over a
  different region (289 → 81 vertices) read the old record against the new
  numbering: 0.078 off a fresh stroke against a 0.08 ceiling. **This is
  reachable through the C ABI** with `clay_multires_remove_highest_level` +
  `clay_multires_add_level_for_patches`. Separately, `set_base_mesh` under a live
  sculptor restarted `cache_generation` onto the value the sculptor last saw, so
  it did not rebind and kept stamping through a freed level mesh — a
  use-after-free, **C++ only** (no binding exposes `set_base_mesh`). A
  process-wide `MultiresSurface::structure_revision()` now decides the rebind.
- **The regional boundary frame is gated** (#638, tests and docs only). The
  frame defect the ROADMAP described was fixed on 2026-09-07 (`f40ee3fe`, #491)
  and first shipped in v0.97.0; nothing gated it. Three cases now compare
  a regional hierarchy with authored rim detail against the dense one.

## What moves under you

### The formats

**`.clayspace` and the scene format do not move**; `kSceneMinor` is unchanged
and nothing written by v0.120.0 reads differently. **No kernel math changed** —
nothing under `include/clay/kernel/`, `include/clay/eval/` or `backends/` moved
since v0.120.0 — so the kernels artifact's parity fixture is unchanged and a
host pinning `claycore-kernels.zip` re-baselines nothing.

**The crash journal gains an event** (#647): a voxel sculpt-layer operation is
journaled as a new event kind appended at the end of the enum, with its own
version byte (`kOpVersion = 1`) and `pass_afters` (#651). Older journals still
decode. The journal is session-private and carries no forward-compatibility
promise: **a journal written by v0.120.1 that records a sculpt-layer operation
is not replayable by v0.120.0.** Recover with the library that wrote it.

### The entry points

- **`clay_tape_info` returns ±FLT_MAX bounds** for a document where an infinite
  grid reaches the result unconfined, and `clay_mesh`, `clay_voxel_rasterize`,
  the bakes, pyclay's three `Volume` constructors and `clay_sdf_smooth_begin`
  **refuse** such a document or layer without a region (#645). Raycast and pick
  now hit every copy.
- **`clay_tape_info`'s box narrows under subtract and intersect and grows by a
  smooth group's ring** (#637). Anything sized from it moves: pyclay
  `Document.mesh(resolution=)` meshes finer wherever the box narrowed.
- **`clay_document_undo_bound` / `_redo_bound` report a much smaller box** for a
  step that adds, removes or changes a head of grab / magnify / blob / alpha
  links: 12 bricks where it was 1,000 / 1,440 / 4,000 at 1 / 10 / 40 grabs
  (#648). Everything else reports what it did.
- **`clay_layer_node_influence_bound` and `clay_brick_cache_mark_dirty_nodes`
  report a wider box** for a node followed by a smooth sibling, by that
  sibling's blend support (#653). Nodes followed only by hard combines are
  bit-identical.
- **A full brick build fills differently** for a repeated item carrying a grab,
  and for a grab behind a magnify, twist or pose (#652): it now matches the raw
  field there.
- **Five voxel sculpt-layer operations, the creation of a sculpt layer, and
  every edit inside one are undo steps** (#647, #651). `clay_document_undo`
  reverses the operation rather than an older step, and `undo_depth` counts
  them. **An unmatched `clay_document_end_undo_group` is a no-op**, and **a
  nested group folds once**, at the outermost.
- **pyclay `MultiresSculptor.stamp("layer")` and `SculptLayerStroke.stamp("layer")`
  move the surface**, up to 0.05 by default (#636, #646).
- **`LayeredMultiresSculptor::smooth` answers differently at the rim of a
  regionally refined hierarchy** (#646): it now agrees with the dense hierarchy.
- **C++ only.** `VoxelGrid::begin_sculpt_layer`, `set_sculpt_layer_strength`,
  `_visible`, `move_sculpt_layer`, `remove_sculpt_layer` and
  `merge_sculpt_layer_down` gain a trailing `SculptLayerOp* record = nullptr`
  (source-compatible, not link-compatible); `session::Step::Kind` gains
  `VoxelLayerProperty`, so an exhaustive `switch` over it needs a case;
  `MultiresSurface::structure_revision()` is new. Recompile.

## Known limits, stated rather than discovered

### From this release

- **#649 is open: a full brick build still disagrees with its own raw field
  inside the band** on some documents, and a Move leaves those bricks stale.
  #652 fixed the deformer cull; two mechanisms remain. (A) The chain cull pad is
  below one blend's support on short chains — the #335 envelope was measured
  only at N ≥ 75 and is applied to smaller layers; one seed reaches 0.031, and
  the error can reach about 0.1k. Repairing it trades against #335's perf win
  and needs its own change. (B) Squashed (non-uniform per-axis scale)
  placements are culled as though their field were a distance. After #652 the
  rich sweep still has 35 documents off, worst 0.0825.
- **Undo's per-brick cost still grows with chain length** (#648): 7.4 → 28.1 µs
  per brick from 1 to 160 grabs (Mac M-series, CPU backend). And these still
  report the node's bound: a radial pose, a head behind a twist or any other
  whole-item link, an easing not exactly zero at its rim on every backend (the
  sines, `out_expo`, the circs, `in_bounce`, `in_out_bounce`), an infinite
  repeat grid, a morph or hidden group above the item.
- **Refilling a baked volume** (~430 µs against ~7 µs per brick, the host's
  numbers) is #639's second route and was not attempted.
- **A GATED node after the edited one is not modelled** by #653's dilation. A
  lerp of two beyond-band values need not stay beyond band; the intersect
  surface delta already refuses that case.
- **`clay_layer_bounds` / `pick::selection_bounds` still report one cell for a
  lattice** (#645). That box promises a camera frame, not material coverage,
  and is left alone.
- **#641: a journal started by a mid-session enable pairs with a stale
  snapshot** and replays without the pre-enable edits (probed: replay reports
  `{'applied': 1}` and the recovered cell reads 0 where the live document has
  1). `docs/05` and `clay.h` tell hosts to save once after enabling.
- **#643: a `clay_mesh_sculptor` stamp on a document mesh layer is not a
  document undo step.** The c-abi scenario that said otherwise was false and now
  says what the ABI records.
- **#644: the journal's write-path cost on the device is unmeasured.** The only
  figures are Linux x86_64 ratios from `unify-the-undo-history` 1.7 — 1.005x on
  `sculpt_smooth`, 1.77x on a bare loop of 64 raw `set` calls, 16 bytes per
  journaled cell — and they are not device numbers.
- **Still not an undo step:** creating a MASK, and dropping a resolution level.
  A voxel sculpt layer's RECORDING state is not saved, and
  `end_sculpt_layer` is not a step.
- **`correct-the-undo-scope` has two open tasks**, and
  `make-voxel-sculpt-layer-creation-a-step` (11 of 11 ticked, #651) is not
  archived.
- **The mixed-depth multires export has no C, pyclay or Swift entry point**
  (`expose-the-mixed-depth-export`, 0 of 8). The C++ export is finished and
  gated; neither known host refines a region.
- **Complexity flagged, not refactored:** `History::apply_step` 68 → 71 and
  `History::replay` 155 → 158 (#647), and `compile_list` at 59 (#637), all
  per-kind switches or already over on v0.120.0.
- **Hosts not named.** #636, #638, #645, #646, #647, #651, #652 report counts
  and byte comparisons from `cpu-only` builds; #653's timings name no host;
  #648 names "Mac (M-series)"; #637 names an Apple M2 Max. This document
  repeats what each says and invents nothing.

### Unchanged from v0.120.0

- **The mesh Grab change (#624) has no device measurement**; the mesh brushes
  are not in the device suite.
- **A fixed mesh has no mitigation for a stretched captured region** (a 1.5
  pull: longest edge 0.1174 → 0.8176), **the adaptive surface reaches past the
  chord on a curve** (115.79%), **the carried region has no upper bound** (45 →
  811 entries on a 1.5 pull-out), and **the collapse protection is measured only
  on sphere grab strokes**.
- **There is no faithful SDF Standard**; building one needs its own proposal —
  now filed as #633. **ClayBuildup is unmeasured.** **Relief's stepping bound
  is poor at stroke density** (L = 46 over 30 dabs), and per-dab
  `move_surface`'s safe step collapses by 1.5x per dab (#632).
- **Adaptive undo is last-in first-out only and nothing compacts**;
  **`serialize` is not the test for "undo restored the surface"**; **a second
  sculptor over the same surface is not kept in step**; **`bytes()` is a
  budget, not an assertion**; **a recorded stroke accumulates into its
  record**.
- **The C++ `session::History` path still leaves a held `DynamicSculptor`'s
  index stale** after an undo — now filed as #629 (200 of 6,912 live faces
  unindexed).
- **The adaptive stroke calls are not in the device coverage gate.**
- **#531 is not closed and the all-brush 16 ms target is unmet.** Nothing in
  this release claims otherwise.
- **#621 has no uncontended timing; native GPU execution of #613/#616/#621/#623
  was not measured; #616's p95 is a small-sample statistic.**
- **Decimation can still emit a non-manifold mesh at some ratios on some
  toolchains** (#575), and **a mesh is not byte-identical across toolchains.**
- **Regional consolidation's local path is narrow by construction.**

## Upgrading

Ordered by how much risk each item carries.

1. **If a document can hold an infinite repeat grid, test `clay_tape_info`'s
   box for ±FLT_MAX and pass a region** to `clay_mesh`,
   `clay_voxel_rasterize`, the bakes and pyclay's `Volume` constructors
   (`bounds=`), or they now refuse (#645). Plan bricks over the view, not the
   box. `clay_sdf_smooth_begin` refuses such a layer outright.
2. **Recount undo steps on voxel sculpt layers** (#647, #651). Strength,
   visibility, move, remove, merge-down, layer creation and every edit inside a
   layer are each a step; a pass in a new layer is two undos. A host mid-pass
   that undoes past the begin must begin the layer again. An unmatched
   `clay_document_end_undo_group` no longer folds the history, and a nested
   group folds once — remove any compensation for either.
3. **If you enable undo mid-session, save once right after** (#641).
4. **Re-pick `resolution=` for resolution-sized meshes** (#637). A subtract or
   intersect now meshes finer at the same resolution, a smooth group's ring can
   make it coarser; at a fixed voxel size nothing changes.
5. **If you dirty bricks from `clay_document_undo_bound` / `_redo_bound` or
   `clay_layer_node_influence_bound`, take the new boxes as they come**
   (#648, #653). The undo box is smaller for Move segments; the node box is
   wider behind a smooth sibling. Do not pad or clip them: the first is proven
   by the identity outside the balls, the second fixes stale bricks.
6. **Re-record any golden that meshes or brick-builds a repeated item with a
   grab, or a grab behind a magnify / twist / pose** (#652). The field there is
   now the raw field.
7. **pyclay scripts calling the multires Layer brush now move the surface**
   (#636, #646), up to 0.05 by default. Pass `layer_height=` to set it.
8. **C++ callers recompile** (`VoxelGrid` out-parameters, `Step::Kind`), and a
   `switch` over `Step::Kind` gains `VoxelLayerProperty`.
9. **Do not replay a v0.120.1 crash journal with v0.120.0** if it recorded a
   sculpt-layer operation.
10. **Nothing to do for `.clayspace`, the scene format, the kernels artifact or
    the C ABI.** No symbol, signature, layout or format minor changed.
