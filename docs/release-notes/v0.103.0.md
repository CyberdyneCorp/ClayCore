# claycore v0.103.0

Six minors since v0.97.0. Two are correctness fixes with a user-visible effect,
one is an interactive-path optimisation, one adds three entry points, and two
are gates that did not exist.

Measured on **iPad15,5 (iPad Air 13-inch, M3), iOS 26.5.2**, gate commit
`db0d9f1b`: 7/7 sessions, 75 cases, `valid: true`, thermal `nominal` throughout.
Mac figures are named as Mac figures wherever they appear.

## What moves under you

### The formats

Nothing. No file-format change, no struct re-layout. `git diff v0.97.0..v0.103.0
-- bindings/c/clay.h` has exactly one `-` line inside a declaration, and it is
the version define.

### The entry points

**`cmagnify_point` and `cblob_offset` change what they return** under four
easing curves — `ease_in_back`, `ease_in_out_back`, `ease_in_elastic`,
`ease_in_out_elastic`. Those curves go negative inside the ball, and that
undershoot is the curve: a negative weight is the deformer acting the opposite
way, which is what they exist for. Both guarded with `if (w <= 0.0f)` and so
early-returned out of it, leaving magnify **inert over the outer 94.9% of the
ball it was asked to act on**. Measured across 4.22M samples on all 33 easings:
the corrected `== 0.0f` form differs from no early-out at **zero** samples; the
old `<= 0.0f` differed at **77,712**, by up to 0.208 world units. A caller using
any other easing sees nothing change. (ABI 0.102.0)

**A no-op `clay_set_layer_mirror` no longer records an undo entry or
invalidates the layer.** Setting the mirror to the axes and `k` it already
carries is now refused as unchanged, the way `SetLayerCompositionCmd` already
did. Measured: 200 bricks dirtied against 0 for doing nothing, 8.88 ms on a
60-item form. A host that relied on a redundant set producing an undo entry will
see one fewer. (ABI 0.103.0)

### Three new entry points

```c
clay_mesh_from_quads        /* store quads a host's own retopologiser produced */
clay_configure_workers      /* size the pool, or ask for none */
clay_worker_report_get      /* what the pool is, without freezing it */
```

## Faster

- **A group drag refills 2.006x fewer bricks** — 4300 -> 2144 over 32 frames,
  450 -> 314 ms, stale bricks 0 both sides. A reach bound was dilated by a
  combine that never happens.
- **A resumed brick rebuilds once instead of 5.56 times** — 28.3 -> 12.2 ms,
  and against the root control at equal brick count 4.6x -> 1.74x. One field
  was not written where the reader takes it.
- **`clay_layer_eval_points` and its siblings cull to the probes they were
  handed** — 12-20x on clustered probes, free within 2% otherwise, and
  bit-identical by construction: the band is verified against the answers, and a
  query reaching past it is re-run with no region at all.
- **`cgrab_point` gains a zero-weight early-out** — about 2x on a
  whole-document evaluation, a few percent on the per-brick interactive path,
  which already runs the deformer cull.
- The worker pool is sized from **performance cores**, not logical ones.

## Gates that did not exist

**`test_deformer_goldens.cpp`** walks every deformer enumerator and pins numeric
field values, with a `static_assert` on the LAST enumerator so an append is a
compile error. Before it, no test anywhere asserted a deformer's values: the
guard meant to force one pinned `cdeform_noise == 13`, an enumerator in the
middle of the enum, so deformers 14-20 were invisible to it. And the CPU/Metal
parity suite cannot catch a kernel change at all, because both backends compile
the same header.

Shown to fail rather than assumed to work. A break that shaves the falloff rim
while preserving finite support:

```
new deformer goldens          1 case failed,    37 assertions failed
pre-existing deformer suite   114 cases passed, 132,011 assertions green
```

**`test_ease_slopes.cpp`** asserts every easing's declared slope is at or above
its measured one — the direction that matters, since a bound too large costs
frames and a bound too small lets the marcher step through the surface.

**`test_c_measure_reach.cpp`** reaches every surface measure from C and pins
that no measure is another measure under a different name.

## Known limits, stated rather than discovered

- **The circ easings declare a Lipschitz slope BELOW their real one** (#543).
  `E'(t) = t/sqrt(1-t^2)` is unbounded at the endpoint, so no sampled value
  bounds it. Pre-existing, found by the new ease-slope test, pinned there as the
  current behaviour. A grab using `ease_in_circ`, `ease_out_circ` or
  `ease_in_out_circ` can be stepped through. **Not fixed in this release.**
- **Deformers 14-20 have no parity scene and are unverified on Metal** (#535).
  The new goldens run the CPU tape only. Unchanged from v0.97.0, and now named.
- **A Move chain's declared bound is about 10^5 times the field's real
  gradient** (#541). At 48 gestures: declared 1,443,068, measured gradient
  4.958 — a slack of 291,049x. The marcher takes far more steps than the field
  requires, and Move degrades across gestures accordingly — 255 ms to 739 ms
  over five dabs, measured in a host. Decomposed since: 139,509x of it is the
  global-max-per-link pricing, 2.23x submultiplicativity, and the composed
  Jacobian IS the field gradient to within 1.01x. **Not fixed in this release.**
- **`ease_max_slope` still prices the polynomial and sine easings from a
  512-point sample times a blanket 1.25x** (#542), rather than their analytic
  suprema. A fix measuring **1,443,068 -> 116,008 (12.44x)** on that same 48-grab
  chain is written and tested but **deliberately held out of this tag**: it
  edits `src/scene/bounds.cpp`, which would invalidate the device gate recorded
  at `db0d9f1b`. It lands in the next minor, gated on its own hardware run.
- **`pose_region`'s device budget is 12.6x its measurement** (#539), so it
  cannot object to a plausible regression.
- **`cut_passes` is scored on the median of eight one-shot timings** (#544), so
  one point moving reads as a regression. Its baseline is re-derived here; the
  scoring is not fixed.
- Every SDF brush stalls on a clean one-item sphere, 63.6 ms to 244.6 ms
  (#530, #531). Most of that is host-side and unattributed.

## Upgrading

1. **If you use magnify or blob with a back or elastic easing, look at your
   output.** It changes, and it changes toward what those curves were always
   supposed to do. Every other easing is bit-identical.
2. **If you carry deformer goldens of your own, check whether they probe INSIDE
   the ball.** Ours did not, for years, and 132,011 assertions passed a kernel
   that was wrong 2.4% inside the rim.
3. `clay_set_layer_mirror` with an unchanged value is now a no-op. If you count
   undo entries, count one fewer.
4. Nothing else requires action. No format change, no re-layout, no removal.
